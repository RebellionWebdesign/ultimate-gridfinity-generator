Privacy Statement

1) Introduction and contact details of the controller

1.1 We are pleased that you are visiting our website and thank you for your interest. Below we inform you about how your personal data is handled when you use our website. Personal data is any data with which you can be personally identified.

1.2 The controller responsible for data processing on this website within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is Daniel Stauffer, Rebellion Webdesign, Feuchtwangerweg 7, 12353 Berlin, Germany, Tel.: +49 30 552 11 964, Email: info@rebellion-webdesign.com. The controller responsible for the processing of personal data is the natural or legal person who, alone or jointly with others, decides on the purposes and means of the processing of personal data.

2) Data collection when visiting our website

2.1 When our website is used for information purposes only, i.e. when you do not register or otherwise send us information, we do not write server log files. So that the website can be displayed to you, your browser transmits data to the server as a technical necessity (for example your IP address, date and time, the page requested, the browser used and the operating system). This data is processed only for the duration of delivery and is not stored: neither by the web server, nor by the container it runs in, nor by the upstream proxy (Traefik). The processing takes place pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing the website.

Only the local development server on the operator's own computer (localhost) writes logs. They relate only to the development of the website and contain no data of visitors.

2.2 For security reasons and to protect the transmission of personal data and other confidential content (e.g. orders or enquiries to us), this website uses SSL or TLS encryption. You can recognise an encrypted connection by the character string "https://" and the lock symbol in your browser line.

3) Cookies and local storage

3.1 This website does not set any cookies.

3.2 The website uses your browser's local storage (Local Storage) and your browser's cache storage (for offline use) only if you click "Allow" in the dialog "Save in this browser?". Before that, no models are saved or read and no service worker is set up. Only your decision in the dialog itself is saved, so that we do not ask you again at every visit.

3.3 If you agree, your browser stores the following: your models (all settings, the text and the SVG or font file you use in the editor), your choice between light and dark display, and the files of the app for offline use. This data does not leave your device. It is not transmitted to us or to third parties.

3.4 The legal basis is your consent pursuant to Section 25(1) of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG). Saving your decision in the dialog is necessary for the dialog to work (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

3.5 You can withdraw your consent at any time: in the editor, click "Clear saved models…" under "In this browser" and then "Switch off saving". This removes the saved models, your display choice, the offline cache and the service worker. You can also delete the website data in your browser's settings.

4) Hosting & content delivery network

For the hosting of our website and the display of the page content, we use a provider that provides its services itself or through selected subcontractors exclusively on servers within the European Union.

All data collected on our website is processed on these servers, unless other data recipients are named below for specific data processing.

We have concluded a data processing agreement with the provider that ensures the protection of our site visitors' data and prohibits unauthorised disclosure to third parties.

5) Google Search Console

We use Google Search Console, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. It shows us how our website appears in Google Search, for example for which search queries it is displayed and how often it is clicked, and whether errors occur when the pages are crawled. We receive this information in aggregated form. Data of individual users of the search engine or of individual visitors to this website cannot be viewed by us.

No code from Google is embedded on this website for this purpose, no cookies are set, and no data is transmitted to Google when the website is called up. To confirm that the website belongs to us, the source code of the home page contains a meta tag with a code that is assigned to us alone.

Google itself collects the pages for the search. Google's privacy policy applies to this (https://policies.google.com/privacy), also with regard to the transfer of data to third countries. We use Search Console on the basis of our legitimate interest in the discoverability and improvement of our website pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

When you contact us (e.g. via contact form or email), personal data is processed, exclusively for the purpose of handling and answering your enquiry and only to the extent necessary for this.

The legal basis for the processing of this data is our legitimate interest in answering your enquiry pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. If your contact aims at a contract, the additional legal basis for the processing is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Your data will be deleted when it can be inferred from the circumstances that the matter in question has been conclusively resolved and provided that no statutory retention obligations stand in the way.

The duration of storage of personal data is determined by the respective legal basis, the purpose of processing and, where applicable, additionally by the respective statutory retention period (e.g. commercial and tax retention periods).

If personal data is processed on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, this data will be stored until you exercise your right to object pursuant to Art. 21(1) GDPR, unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for the processing that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves to assert, exercise or defend legal claims.

Unless the other information in this statement on specific processing situations provides otherwise, stored personal data will otherwise be deleted when it is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected or otherwise processed.
